THE STUDIO

From one game to the next

DEXPit starts with cards.

Players open an arena pack. The HexPit launcher records what they own and how much GAME they can claim. Flap opens the first market when the round fills.

A second game could start with gear.

Another Engines of Creation title could adapt the item catalog and sale rules, then reuse the stablecoin collection, factory pattern and pool settlement. A second title is our proposed next test.
